Soundbar Pro

Re‑establishing Apple in the smart‑home market.

01 — Market Entry

HomePod entered a market
Apple didn't lead.

In 2018, Apple released its first smart speaker, the HomePod, aiming to compete in the growing smart‑home market established by Amazon's Echo and Google's Google Home — positioned as a premium, music‑first alternative to mass‑market voice assistants.

Apple said the product "did well." The numbers tell a different story.

02 — The Problem

Apple followed the category.
It never redefined it.

Instead of redefining the category, HomePod followed it — using a plastic, rounded design already familiar from competitor products.

Apple had an opportunity to bring its own premium design language to the smart‑home market. But they didn't. Apple's iconic design principles were left at home — missing the chance to introduce a premium, luxury alternative to smart‑home speakers.
